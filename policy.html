Privacy Policy

Your Privacy Matters

WAKALEBIRE is designed as a completely offline visual memory game. Your privacy is respected by design. The game does not require an account, does not request personal information, and does not collect or transmit your data.

📴

Works Offline

No internet connection is required.

🔒

No Sign In

No account creation or login.

🛡️

No Data Collection

No personal information is collected.

🚫

No Permissions

The game requests no device permissions.

Information We Collect

WAKALEBIRE does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. No names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, photos, location, or device identifiers are collected.

Game Data

The game stores only your highest score locally on your device so that your progress can be remembered between sessions. This information never leaves your device and is never transmitted anywhere.

Permissions

WAKALEBIRE does not request access to:

  • Location
  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Photos or Media
  • Contacts
  • SMS
  • Phone Calls
  • Bluetooth
  • Storage
  • Notifications

Internet Connectivity

The game is fully functional without an internet connection. No online services, cloud storage, analytics, advertising, or external servers are used.

Children's Privacy

WAKALEBIRE is intended for players aged 13 years and above. The application does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Third-Party Services

WAKALEBIRE does not integrate advertising networks, analytics platforms, social media services, or third-party data collection services.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the application changes in future releases. Any updates will appear on this page together with the revised effective date.
